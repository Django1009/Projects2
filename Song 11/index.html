Overview

My trip to the LSC was incredible. The exhibits were all very unique and educational. There was even a section where they had a live snake play in a ballpit. It was very cool to watch. Then, I saw a different animal in the same exibit that I had never seen before. It is called a Degu. I have a picture of one in the photo gallery. It is so cute! Other than that, the trip was pretty normal. Science stuff, basic coding, a planetarium experience. It was pretty amazing.

Top Three Highlights

  1. Skeleton
  2. Seeing the Degu (cutest thing i ever seen).
  3. The big rollercoaster in the planitarium that almost made Kenny sick.

RATING

★★★★☆ Stars - I really liked it and wish we could have spent more time there, but it feels like we spent too long looking around places that werent really interesting.

I didnt get enough photos for a photo gallery :(
